
| Function                         | Responsibility                                                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project Manager                  | Determines overall project staffing requirements              |
| Technical Lead                   | Determines technical roles and required technical skills      |
| Requirements/Business Analyst    | Helps determine business and functional staffing requirements |
| Talent/HR Manager                | Identifies qualified and available personnel                  |
| Project Manager + Technical Lead | Select suitable candidates                                    |
| Management/Admin                 | Approves the final team                                       |

# 4. DETERMINING PROJECT STAFFING REQUIREMENTS

Personnel requirements shall be determined after the project's requirements, technical feasibility, architecture, and documentation have been completed.

The project shall contain an approved **Staffing Plan**.

For example:

### Project: Hospital Management System

```text
Project Manager              1
Business Analyst             1
UI/UX Designer               1
Frontend Developer           2
Backend Developer            2
Database Engineer            1
QA Engineer                  1
DevOps Engineer              1
Security Engineer            1
```

The number of personnel required shall be justified by:

* Project scope;
* System complexity;
* Development workload;
* Project deadline;
* Required technologies;
* Security requirements;
* Testing requirements;
* Deployment requirements;
* Budget;
* Project risks.

The staffing requirement shall not be created arbitrarily.

---

The expected period of participation shall be recorded.

# 9. EXPRESSION OF INTEREST

Where N.O.U. chooses to allow personnel to volunteer for projects, the process shall use an **Expression of Interest** mechanism.

A qualified employee may select:

> **Express Interest**

The individual remains a candidate until the project management process is completed.

This prevents project positions from being awarded solely on a first-come-first-served basis.

# 10. CANDIDATE SELECTION

Candidate selection shall normally be performed by the:

**Project Manager + Technical Lead**

with assistance from Talent/HR where necessary.

Selection shall consider:

* Technical suitability;
* Assessment performance;
* Relevant experience;
* Availability;
* Current workload;
* Previous project performance;
* Communication ability;
* Project requirements;
* Team balance.

The objective is to create a team capable of successfully delivering the project.

---

# 11. MAXIMUM ACTIVE PROJECT LIMIT

An N.O.U. personnel member shall normally participate in a maximum of **three active projects simultaneously**.

The system shall automatically track project assignments.

Example:

the person may be considered for another project.

Any exception to the three-project limit shall require authorized management approval and shall be recorded in the system.

# 12. TEAM LEADER APPOINTMENT

The first person to express interest in a project shall **not automatically become Team Leader**.

Team leadership shall be based on suitability for leadership.

The appointment may consider:

* Technical competence;
* Relevant experience;
* Communication;
* Leadership ability;
* Previous project performance;
* Reliability;
* Knowledge of the project;
* Availability.

The Team Leader shall be appointed by the Project Manager or authorized management.

---

# 13. PROJECT MANAGER AND TEAM LEADER

The positions of Project Manager and Team Leader shall be treated as separate responsibilities.

### Project Manager

Responsible for:

* Project schedule;
* Resources;
* Project coordination;
* Customer communication;
* Risks;
* Deadlines;
* Overall project delivery;
* Project reporting.

### Team Leader

Responsible for:

* Day-to-day team coordination;
* Task distribution;
* Monitoring team progress;
* Identifying technical blockers;
* Internal team communication;
* Reporting team progress;
* Preparing weekly project progress reports.

The Team Leader reports project/team progress to the Project Manager or authorized administrator.

---

# 17. PROJECT CONFIDENTIALITY

Project visibility shall be controlled throughout the staffing process.

Before staffing:

> **Project remains confidential and is not visible to general personnel.**

During recruitment:

> **Eligible personnel may see limited project information necessary to determine whether they wish to express interest.**

After selection:

> **Assigned personnel receive project-specific access.**

This prevents unnecessary disclosure of:

* Customer information;
* Customer business processes;
* Pricing;
* Confidential documentation;
* Technical designs;
* Source code;
* Business strategies.

# 18. INSUFFICIENT PERSONNEL

If the required number of qualified personnel cannot be obtained, the project shall not automatically proceed into development.

For example:

```text
Required Backend Developers: 2
Available Qualified Developers: 1
```

The project shall be marked:

> **STAFFING INCOMPLETE**

The Project Manager/Talent Manager may:

* Search the Talent Pool again;
* Wait for personnel to become available;
* Recruit additional personnel;
* Reassign personnel where appropriate;
* Request management approval for an exception.

Development should begin only when the required staffing conditions have been satisfied or an authorized exception has been approved.

---

# 19. STAFFING OVERRIDE

In exceptional circumstances, management may authorize a project to proceed with fewer personnel than originally planned.

Any override must document:

* Reason for the exception;
* Personnel shortage;
* Impact on the project;
* Risk;
* Revised timeline;
* Person approving the exception.

The system shall record the decision in the project audit log.

# 27. GOVERNING RULE

N.O.U. shall operate under the following rule:

> **No individual shall be assigned to a project solely on the basis of interest or availability. Project membership shall be based on the approved staffing requirements, professional classification, demonstrated skills, availability, project workload, performance, and management approval.**

Furthermore:

> **The system shall recommend qualified personnel, but final team formation shall remain an authorized management decision.**

This structure allows N.O.U. to maintain proper control while the company is small and provides a scalable foundation for the future recruitment of Project Managers, Technical Leads, Talent/HR personnel, Team Leaders, and other management functions.

# 1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes how personnel who successfully complete the N.O.U. employment assessment are admitted into the N.O.U. professional environment.

The system is designed to provide qualified personnel with:

* A centralized N.O.U. account;
* Access to their professional portal;
* Access to the N.O.U. Community;
* A professional profile;
* Access to suitable project opportunities;
* Participation in assigned projects;
* Access to professional development activities;
* A controlled communication and collaboration environment.

The purpose is to ensure that personnel remain connected to N.O.U. even when they are not currently assigned to a development project.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

N.O.U. shall use a centralized identity and access model.

> **Personnel shall use one N.O.U. account to access the services and platforms for which they are authorized.**

The system shall determine access based on:

* Employment status;
* Professional classification;
* Qualifications;
* Assigned role;
* Project membership;
* Permissions;
* Administrative authorization.

Personnel shall not be required to maintain separate usernames and passwords for every N.O.U. platform unless a specific security requirement makes separate authentication necessary.

---
