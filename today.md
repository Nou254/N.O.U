## 1. Introduction

N.O.U. Digital Systems will provide a centralized digital platform through which software, applications, websites, digital tools, and technology solutions developed by N.O.U. may be presented, distributed, licensed, or made available for download.

To make the platform organized and easy to navigate, software products will be classified according to their **primary purpose, target users, and area of application**.

A product may belong to one primary category while also carrying additional tags describing its features and supported platforms.

The following categories establish the initial classification structure for N.O.U.

# 1. INTRODUCTION

N.O.U. Digital Systems shall operate a structured employment assessment system through the N.O.U. employment platform.

The assessment system is designed to determine not only whether an applicant has memorized technical information, but whether the applicant can:

* Think critically;
* Solve real-world problems;
* Apply technical knowledge;
* Understand professional concepts;
* Design practical solutions;
* Troubleshoot problems;
* Make appropriate decisions;
* Work within technical and business requirements; and
* Demonstrate the competence required for the position applied for.

The assessment shall therefore combine **general reasoning, field-specific education, technical knowledge, practical exercises and professional judgement**.

---

# 2. ASSESSMENT PHILOSOPHY

N.O.U. shall not use one examination for every applicant.

A person applying for Cybersecurity should not receive the same technical examination as a UI/UX Designer, while a Project Manager should not be assessed in the same manner as a Backend Developer.

The assessment system shall therefore contain:

### A. Common Assessment

Administered to applicants across most professional fields.

### B. Category Assessment

Tests knowledge of the applicant's selected professional field.

### C. Position Assessment

Tests knowledge specific to the particular position.

### D. Practical Assessment

Requires the applicant to perform an actual task.

### E. Professional Assessment

Evaluates communication, ethics, teamwork, decision-making and professional conduct where applicable.

---

# 3. LOGIC AND CRITICAL THINKING ASSESSMENT

## 3.1 Logic Assessment Principle

The N.O.U. Logic Assessment shall **not primarily consist of mathematical puzzles, IQ-style questions or memorization**.

It shall primarily consist of **real-world problems requiring applicants to understand a situation, identify the problem, evaluate available information, consider constraints and develop an appropriate solution.**

For example:

> A company's website works correctly for employees inside the office but becomes extremely slow when accessed by customers from outside the network. The server has sufficient CPU and memory. What would you investigate first, and why?

The applicant should explain their reasoning rather than simply select an answer.

---

# 4. COMMON LOGIC MODULES

The following logic areas may be used across the N.O.U. assessment system.

### Logic Module 1: Problem Identification

Applicants are given a real-world problem and must identify the actual problem rather than the obvious symptom.

### Logic Module 2: Critical Decision-Making

Applicants evaluate several possible decisions and explain which one is most appropriate.

### Logic Module 3: Resource Constraints

Problems include limitations such as:

* Time;
* Budget;
* Personnel;
* Hardware;
* Internet connectivity;
* Security;
* Infrastructure.

Applicants must develop practical solutions within the constraints.

### Logic Module 4: Troubleshooting

Applicants receive a system failure or operational problem and must determine how they would investigate it.

### Logic Module 5: Cause-and-Effect Reasoning

Applicants determine how one event affects another.

### Logic Module 6: Prioritization

Applicants receive multiple problems and must determine which should be handled first and why.

### Logic Module 7: Risk Analysis

Applicants identify potential risks associated with a proposed solution.

### Logic Module 8: Real-World Solution Design

Applicants are presented with a practical scenario and must propose a complete solution.

---

# 41. Important Rule for N.O.U.

The assessment system should **not be designed merely to eliminate people**.

Its more important purpose is to answer:

> **"What can this person actually do, and where can N.O.U. use their abilities?"**

Therefore, an applicant who does not qualify for Backend Development might demonstrate excellent UI/UX, networking, database, technical support, or analytical abilities.

The system should be capable of recommending an alternative category where the assessment evidence supports it.

---

# 42. Final Assessment Principle

N.O.U. Digital Systems shall apply the following principle:

> **Knowledge gets an applicant into consideration; practical ability demonstrates competence; critical thinking demonstrates problem-solving capacity; professional conduct demonstrates reliability; and project performance demonstrates sustained capability.**

The assessment system should therefore become a **talent classification engine**, not simply an employment examination.

**Next logical document:** *Position-by-Position Assessment Structure* — where we take each category above and define the actual positions under it, the required educational modules, number/type of questions, practical examination, pass marks, and how the system determines whether an applicant qualifies for that particular position.

# 1. PURPOSE

The N.O.U. Digital Systems project management process establishes the controlled procedure through which a customer project moves from the initial request to analysis, technical assessment, documentation, approval, team formation, development, testing, deployment, and eventual closure.

The purpose of this process is to ensure that:

* Customer requests are properly evaluated before development begins.
* Projects are technically and commercially feasible.
* Requirements are clearly documented before coding begins.
* Appropriate personnel are selected based on their qualifications and availability.
* Confidential customer information is protected.
* Developers do not receive access to projects before they are ready for development.
* Every project has clearly defined responsibilities, deadlines, documentation, and approval points.
* Project changes are controlled and documented.
* Management maintains appropriate oversight throughout the project lifecycle.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

N.O.U. shall operate on the following principle:

> **A customer request is not automatically a development project.**

A request must pass through the appropriate evaluation, analysis, documentation, technical review, and approval stages before it becomes an active development project.

Similarly:

> **Personnel shall not receive access to a customer project merely because they are qualified developers.**

Project access shall be granted according to:

1. Professional classification;
2. Required skills;
3. Availability;
4. Project assignment;
5. Role within the project; and
6. Access permissions.

# 4. STAGE ONE — CUSTOMER PROJECT REQUEST

For example, a customer may state:

N.O.U. shall determine the appropriate technical solution through the analysis process.

# 5. STAGE TWO — ADMIN INITIAL ASSESSMENT

Once submitted, the request enters the **Admin Project Intake Portal**.

At this stage, the project is **confidential and restricted**.

* Completeness of the request;
* Customer information;
* Nature of the requested service;
* Business suitability;
* Initial technical indication;
* Potential legal or compliance concerns;
* Whether the project falls within N.O.U.'s services.

# 6. STAGE THREE — REQUIREMENTS AND BUSINESS ANALYSIS

An approved project is assigned to the **Requirements & Systems Analysis function**.

The purpose of this stage is to determine exactly what the customer requires.

The analyst shall establish:

### Functional Requirements

What the system must do.

### Non-Functional Requirements

How the system should operate.

### User Roles

Who will use the system.

### Business Rules

Rules governing how the customer's organization operates.

### Workflows

How information and activities move through the system.

### Acceptance Criteria

Conditions that must be satisfied before the customer can accept the completed system.

The analyst may communicate directly with the customer through the authorized project communication channel.

---

# 7. STAGE FOUR — TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY ASSESSMENT

Once the requirements are sufficiently understood, the project moves to technical assessment.

The technical function determines whether the proposed system can reasonably be developed by N.O.U.

The assessment may consider:

* Required technologies;
* Software architecture;
* Database requirements;
* APIs and external services;
* Infrastructure;
* Security;
* Scalability;
* Performance;
* Integration requirements;
* Development complexity;
* Technical risks;
* Availability of required skills.

The result shall be classified as:

```text
TECHNICALLY FEASIBLE
TECHNICALLY FEASIBLE WITH MODIFICATIONS
REQUIRES FURTHER ANALYSIS
NOT CURRENTLY FEASIBLE
```

A project that is not feasible should not proceed directly into development.

# 8. STAGE FIVE — SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

If the project is technically feasible, the technical team prepares the proposed architecture.

* System architecture;

* API architecture;
* Frontend architecture;
* Backend architecture;
* Authentication architecture;
* Security architecture;
* Infrastructure architecture;
* Integration architecture;
* Backup and recovery strategy.

The architecture establishes **how the system is expected to work before developers begin implementing it.**

The N.O.U. Project Staffing and Team Formation Policy establishes the procedures through which qualified personnel are identified, evaluated, selected, assigned, and organized into teams for approved company projects.

The purpose of this policy is to ensure that every project receives personnel whose skills, experience, availability, and professional classification correspond to the requirements of the project.

Team formation shall be based on the needs of the project and the qualifications of personnel rather than on personal preference, first-come-first-served selection, or arbitrary assignment.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

N.O.U. shall operate under the following principle:

> **The project determines the personnel required, and the qualifications of personnel determine who is eligible to participate.**

Personnel shall not be assigned to projects solely because they express interest.

Similarly, being a qualified N.O.U. developer does not automatically give an individual access to every project.

Project participation shall be determined by:

* Professional classification;
* Assessment results;
* Technical skills;
* Experience;
* Availability;
* Current project workload;
* Previous performance;
* Project requirements;
* Leadership capability where applicable.

---

# 3. RESPONSIBILITY FOR TEAM FORMATION

Team formation shall primarily be a management function supported by the N.O.U. Talent Management system.

The principal responsibilities shall be divided as follows:

| Function                         | Responsibility                                                |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Project Manager                  | Determines overall project staffing requirements              |
| Technical Lead                   | Determines technical roles and required technical skills      |
| Requirements/Business Analyst    | Helps determine business and functional staffing requirements |
| Talent/HR Manager                | Identifies qualified and available personnel                  |
| Project Manager + Technical Lead | Select suitable candidates                                    |
| Management/Admin                 | Approves the final team                                       |
| Project Manager/Management       | Appoints Team Leader                                          |
| System Administrator             | Grants appropriate system permissions                         |
| Team Leader                      | Coordinates the team after formation                          |

During the initial stage of N.O.U., where the Company is operated by one person, the **Super Administrator may perform all of these functions**.

The system shall nevertheless maintain them as separate organizational responsibilities so that they can later be delegated.

---

# 4. DETERMINING PROJECT STAFFING REQUIREMENTS

Personnel requirements shall be determined after the project's requirements, technical feasibility, architecture, and documentation have been completed.

The project shall contain an approved **Staffing Plan**.

For example:

### Project: Hospital Management System

```text
Project Manager              1
Business Analyst             1
UI/UX Designer               1
Frontend Developer           2
Backend Developer            2
Database Engineer            1
QA Engineer                  1
DevOps Engineer              1
Security Engineer            1
```

The number of personnel required shall be justified by:

* Project scope;
* System complexity;
* Development workload;
* Project deadline;
* Required technologies;
* Security requirements;
* Testing requirements;
* Deployment requirements;
* Budget;
* Project risks.

The staffing requirement shall not be created arbitrarily.

* Reliability;

* Confidential documentation;
* Technical designs;
* Source code;

# 18. INSUFFICIENT PERSONNEL

If the required number of qualified personnel cannot be obtained, the project shall not automatically proceed into development.

For example:

```text
Required Backend Developers: 2
Available Qualified Developers: 1
```

The project shall be marked:

> **STAFFING INCOMPLETE**

The Project Manager/Talent Manager may:

* Search the Talent Pool again;
* Wait for personnel to become available;
* Recruit additional personnel;
* Reassign personnel where appropriate;
* Request management approval for an exception.

Development should begin only when the required staffing conditions have been satisfied or an authorized exception has been approved.

Any override must document:

* Reason for the exception;
* Personnel shortage;
* Impact on the project;
* Risk;
* Revised timeline;
* Person approving the exception.

The system shall record the decision in the project audit log.

# 1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes how personnel who successfully complete the N.O.U. employment assessment are admitted into the N.O.U. professional environment.

The system is designed to provide qualified personnel with:

* A centralized N.O.U. account;
* Access to their professional portal;
* Access to the N.O.U. Community;
* A professional profile;
* Access to suitable project opportunities;
* Participation in assigned projects;
* Access to professional development activities;
* A controlled communication and collaboration environment.

The purpose is to ensure that personnel remain connected to N.O.U. even when they are not currently assigned to a development project.

---

# 2. CORE PRINCIPLE

N.O.U. shall use a centralized identity and access model.

> **Personnel shall use one N.O.U. account to access the services and platforms for which they are authorized.**

The system shall determine access based on:

* Employment status;
* Professional classification;
* Qualifications;
* Assigned role;
* Project membership;
* Permissions;
* Administrative authorization.

Personnel shall not be required to maintain separate usernames and passwords for every N.O.U. platform unless a specific security requirement makes separate authentication necessary.

# 1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the official procedure through which a customer requests a software project from N.O.U. * Projects are technically and commercially evaluated before development;
* Customers receive clear quotations;
* Payment obligations are transparent;
* Development teams do not begin unauthorized work;
* Project scope is controlled;
* Customer and N.O.U. interests are protected;
* All project activities are recorded within the N.O.U. platform.

# 5. INITIAL ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW

* Whether the request falls within N.O.U.'s services;
* Whether the request is sufficiently clear;
* Whether there are obvious legal or ethical concerns;
* Whether additional information is required;
* Whether the project appears suitable for N.O.U.

The administrator may:

### Approve for Technical Review

Move the request forward.

### Request Clarification

Return the request to the customer for additional information.

### Decline

Reject the request with an appropriate reason.

# 6. TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY REVIEW

A project that passes the initial administrative review shall undergo technical feasibility evaluation.

The technical review shall determine whether N.O.U. can reasonably develop and support the proposed solution.

The review may consider:

* Required technologies;
* System complexity;
* Security requirements;
* Infrastructure requirements;
* Third-party integrations;
* Database requirements;
* Development resources;
* Required skills;
* Estimated development period;
* Technical risks;
* Legal or regulatory requirements;
* Deployment requirements;
* Long-term maintenance requirements.

The result shall be recorded in the project record.

# 7. TECHNICAL DECISION

The project may receive one of the following decisions:

### Technically Feasible

The project proceeds to documentation.

### Technically Feasible With Conditions

The project can proceed if specified conditions are satisfied.

### Clarification Required

Additional information is required.

### Not Technically Feasible

The project is declined or returned for modification.

The customer shall be informed of the appropriate outcome.

# 8. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: FEASIBILITY IS NOT PROJECT ACCEPTANCE

Approval during technical review shall not automatically mean that N.O.U. has agreed to develop the project.

The status:

> **Technically Feasible**

> N.O.U. believes the proposed system can potentially be developed.

It does not mean:

> N.O.U. has entered into a development contract.

Formal project acceptance occurs after the appropriate documentation, quotation, agreement, and required initial payment have been completed.

N.O.U. Digital Systems shall not rely on manually prepared quotations as the primary long-term process.

The **documentation team shall prepare the project information and commercial estimate**, while the **N.O.U. platform shall generate, calculate, version, store, and present the formal quotation**.

No quotation shall be presented to a customer until the required internal review and approval have been completed.

No customer shall be billed for the project until the applicable quotation and terms have been presented and accepted.

No project shall enter active development until the required agreement and initial payment conditions have been satisfied.

> **Documentation defines the project.
> Administration approves the project.
> The system generates the quotation.
> The customer accepts the terms.
> Billing requests payment.
> Payment verification activates the project.**

Instead of creating completely separate accounts for every portal, a user shall have a central N.O.U. account.

The system shall then determine the user's role and authorized access.

```text
                    N.O.U. ACCOUNT
                          │
        ┌─────────────────┼─────────────────┐
        │                 │                 │
     CUSTOMER          PERSONNEL          INVESTOR
        │                 │                 │
        ▼                 ▼                 ▼
 CUSTOMER PORTAL    PERSONNEL PORTAL   INVESTOR PORTAL
```

Additional authorized roles shall include internal administrative and operational personnel.

---

# 5.25 Central Login

The Home Screen shall provide a central **LOGIN** function.

After authentication, the system shall determine the user's authorized destination.

For example:

```text
CUSTOMER
   ↓
CUSTOMER PORTAL

DEVELOPER
   ↓
PERSONNEL PORTAL

INVESTOR
   ↓
INVESTOR PORTAL

DOCUMENTATION PERSONNEL
   ↓
DOCUMENTATION PORTAL

ADMINISTRATOR
   ↓
ADMINISTRATION PORTAL
```

Users shall not be granted access merely because they know the URL of another portal.

Authorization shall be enforced by the backend.

# 5.26 Administrative Access

The administrator shall have centralized access to the N.O.U. system.

During the initial stage, where N.O.U. is operated by a single administrator, the administrator may have access to all operational areas.

These may include:

* Customer management;
* Personnel management;
* Employment assessments;
* Documentation;
* Project management;
* Product catalogue;
* Investor management;
* Finance;
* System monitoring;
* Security;
* Reports.

As N.O.U. grows, administrative privileges shall be divided according to responsibility and the principle of least privilege.

# 5.36 Governing Principle

The N.O.U. Central Home Screen shall function as the **single public gateway to N.O.U. Digital Systems**.

It shall combine three major public functions:

### 1. N.O.U. SOFTWARE CATALOGUE

A place where users discover, access and download approved N.O.U. software.

### 2. N.O.U. TECHNOLOGY SERVICES

A place where customers can discover N.O.U. services and request custom software or technology solutions.

### 3. N.O.U. ECOSYSTEM

A gateway through which customers, employees and investors can register and access their respective portals.

The Home Screen shall therefore establish **nou.com as the central digital platform of N.O.U. Digital Systems**, while keeping the company's internal operational systems separated behind authentication and role-based access control.
