# 33. GOVERNING RULE

N.O.U. Digital Systems shall not rely on manually prepared quotations as the primary long-term process.

The **documentation team shall prepare the project information and commercial estimate**, while the **N.O.U. platform shall generate, calculate, version, store, and present the formal quotation**.

No quotation shall be presented to a customer until the required internal review and approval have been completed.

No customer shall be billed for the project until the applicable quotation and terms have been presented and accepted.

No project shall enter active development until the required agreement and initial payment conditions have been satisfied.

**This creates a controlled chain of responsibility:**

> **Documentation defines the project.
> Administration approves the project.
> The system generates the quotation.
> The customer accepts the terms.
> Billing requests payment.
> Payment verification activates the project.**

# 5.36 Governing Principle

The N.O.U. Central Home Screen shall function as the **single public gateway to N.O.U. Digital Systems**.

It shall combine three major public functions:

### 1. N.O.U. SOFTWARE CATALOGUE

A place where users discover, access and download approved N.O.U. software.

### 2. N.O.U. TECHNOLOGY SERVICES

A place where customers can discover N.O.U. services and request custom software or technology solutions.

### 3. N.O.U. ECOSYSTEM

A gateway through which customers, employees and investors can register and access their respective portals.

The Home Screen shall therefore establish **nou.com as the central digital platform of N.O.U. Digital Systems**, while keeping the company's internal operational systems separated behind authentication and role-based access control.
