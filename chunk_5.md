# 1. PURPOSE

This policy establishes the official procedure through which a customer requests a software project from N.O.U. Digital Systems, from the initial submission of the request through technical review, documentation, quotation, payment, project activation, development, milestone payments, and final delivery.

The purpose of this process is to ensure that:

* Customer requirements are properly understood;
* Projects are technically and commercially evaluated before development;
* Customers receive clear quotations;
* Payment obligations are transparent;
* Development teams do not begin unauthorized work;
* Project scope is controlled;
* Customer and N.O.U. interests are protected;
* All project activities are recorded within the N.O.U. platform.

---

# 2. CUSTOMER PROJECT REQUEST

A customer may request the development of an application, website, software system, mobile application, PWA, game, business system, or other approved technology solution through the N.O.U. customer portal.

The customer shall provide sufficient information to allow N.O.U. to understand the proposed project.

The request may include:

* Project name;
* Type of application;
* Description of the proposed system;
* Business problem being addressed;
* Required features;
* Intended users;
* Preferred platform;
* Expected integrations;
* Supporting documents;
* Images or reference materials;
* Expected delivery requirements;
* Other relevant information.

---

# 3. PROJECT REQUEST REGISTRATION

Once submitted, the N.O.U. system shall create a unique project request number.

Example:

**NOU-REQ-2026-0048**

The request shall initially be assigned the status:

> **SUBMITTED**

The customer shall be able to view the request from the customer portal.

```text

     ↓
```

# 5. INITIAL ADMINISTRATIVE REVIEW

When a project request is submitted, it shall appear in the authorized N.O.U. administrative portal.

The administrator shall review the request to determine whether it should proceed to technical evaluation.

The administrator shall consider:

* Whether the request falls within N.O.U.'s services;
* Whether the request is sufficiently clear;
* Whether there are obvious legal or ethical concerns;
* Whether additional information is required;
* Whether the project appears suitable for N.O.U.

The administrator may:

### Approve for Technical Review

Move the request forward.

### Request Clarification

Return the request to the customer for additional information.

### Decline

Reject the request with an appropriate reason.

---

# 6. TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY REVIEW

A project that passes the initial administrative review shall undergo technical feasibility evaluation.

The technical review shall determine whether N.O.U. can reasonably develop and support the proposed solution.

The review may consider:

* Required technologies;
* System complexity;
* Security requirements;
* Infrastructure requirements;
* Third-party integrations;
* Database requirements;
* Development resources;
* Required skills;
* Estimated development period;
* Technical risks;
* Legal or regulatory requirements;
* Deployment requirements;
* Long-term maintenance requirements.

The result shall be recorded in the project record.

---

# 7. TECHNICAL DECISION

The project may receive one of the following decisions:

### Technically Feasible

The project proceeds to documentation.

### Technically Feasible With Conditions

The project can proceed if specified conditions are satisfied.

### Clarification Required

Additional information is required.

### Not Technically Feasible

The project is declined or returned for modification.

The customer shall be informed of the appropriate outcome.

---

# 8. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: FEASIBILITY IS NOT PROJECT ACCEPTANCE

Approval during technical review shall not automatically mean that N.O.U. has agreed to develop the project.

The status:

> **Technically Feasible**

means:

> N.O.U. believes the proposed system can potentially be developed.

It does not mean:

> N.O.U. has entered into a development contract.

Formal project acceptance occurs after the appropriate documentation, quotation, agreement, and required initial payment have been completed.

---

Once a project is determined to be technically feasible, it shall move into the N.O.U. project documentation process.
