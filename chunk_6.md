### 9. Activation

The project is formally authorized to enter development.

### 10. Development

The N.O.U. project team performs the work.

This distinction is essential for proper project governance.

# 38. GOVERNING RULE

The N.O.U. customer project system shall operate under the following principle:

> **No customer project shall enter active development merely because a request has been submitted or a quotation has been viewed.**

A project shall proceed to development only after the required internal review, documentation, quotation, customer acceptance, applicable agreement, and required initial payment have been completed and verified.

> **The customer's payment schedule shall be determined by the approved project agreement, with a 30% initial deposit serving as the standard default unless a different arrangement is formally approved.**

This provides N.O.U. with a controlled process from the moment a customer says **"I need an application"** to the point where the development team is formally authorized to build it.

# N.O.U. DIGITAL SYSTEMS

## PROJECT DOCUMENTATION, COST ESTIMATION AND QUOTATION PROCEDURE

**Website:** nou.com

# 1. PURPOSE

This procedure establishes how N.O.U. Digital Systems shall transform an approved customer project request into formal project documentation, cost estimation, quotation, customer agreement, and billing.

The objective is to ensure that every project is:

* Properly understood before development;
* Technically documented;
* Properly estimated;
* Assigned appropriate personnel;
* Commercially evaluated;
* Presented to the customer in a clear quotation;
* Subject to approved terms and conditions;
* Properly recorded in the N.O.U. system.

The process also establishes the responsibilities of the documentation team, administration, finance, and the N.O.U. platform.

---

# 2. PRINCIPLE

N.O.U. shall not prepare a customer quotation based solely on the customer's original request.

The quotation shall be based on an **approved project assessment and documentation process**.

The basic principle is:

> **Customer Request → Technical Review → Documentation → Resource Estimation → Cost Estimation → Internal Approval → Quotation → Customer Agreement → Billing → Payment → Project Activation**

---

# 3. RESPONSIBILITY OF THE DOCUMENTATION TEAM

The documentation team is responsible for converting an approved customer request into a structured project definition.

The documentation team shall determine, where applicable:

* What is being developed;
* Why it is being developed;
* Who will use it;
* What functions it must perform;
* What technologies may be required;
* What infrastructure may be required;
* What personnel are required;
* How long development may take;
* What risks exist;
* What the estimated development cost will be;
* What deliverables N.O.U. is expected to provide.

The documentation team does **not automatically have authority to bind N.O.U. to a final commercial price** unless that authority has been formally assigned.

---

# 4. PROJECT DOCUMENTATION

The documentation team shall prepare the project documentation before the final quotation is generated.

The documentation may contain:

## 4.1 Project Identification

* Project ID;
* Project name;
* Customer;
* Date received;
* Documentation version;
* Assigned documentation team;
* Project category.

## 4.2 Project Overview

A clear explanation of:

* Customer's problem;
* Proposed solution;
* Purpose of the system;
* Intended users;
* Expected outcome.

## 4.3 Functional Requirements

The team shall document the functions that the system is expected to perform.

For example:

* Registration;
* Authentication;
* Dashboard;
* Payments;
* Reporting;
* Notifications;
* Administration;
* Search;
* Data management.

## 4.4 Non-Functional Requirements

The documentation may specify:

* Performance;
* Security;
* Availability;
* Scalability;
* Accessibility;
* Reliability;
* Backup requirements;
* Data protection requirements.

---

# 5. PROJECT SCOPE

The documentation team shall clearly define the project scope.

The project document shall identify:

### Included

Functions and services that are included in the quotation.

### Excluded

Functions and services that are not included.

This distinction is mandatory for projects where unclear scope could result in disputes.

---

# 6. TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

The documentation team shall identify the expected technical requirements.

These may include:

* Frontend;
* Backend;
* Database;
* APIs;
* Cloud infrastructure;
* Hosting;
* Authentication;
* Payment integrations;
* SMS services;
* Email services;
* Third-party services;
* Security systems;
* Mobile applications;
* PWAs;
* Desktop applications;
* Hardware integrations.

The technology selected shall be based on the project requirements rather than personal preference.

# 10. INTERNAL COST VERSUS CUSTOMER PRICE

N.O.U. shall distinguish between:

### Internal Project Cost

The estimated amount N.O.U. expects to spend in delivering the project.

### Customer Project Price

The amount N.O.U. charges the customer.

These values should not automatically be identical.

The customer price may account for:

* N.O.U. operating costs;
* Management;
* Infrastructure;
* Risk;
* Business margin;
* Support;
* Warranty;
* Administrative costs;
* Other approved commercial considerations.

The internal cost estimate should remain restricted to authorized N.O.U. personnel.

* Requirements;
* Scope;
* Technical feasibility;
* Personnel requirements;
* Estimated duration;
* Estimated cost;
* Risks;
* Proposed customer price;
* Payment structure;
* Special conditions.
